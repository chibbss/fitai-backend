# Beta Tester Signup Form Template

## Dead Simple Form - 4 Questions, 1 Minute

**Goal**: Get all 20 testers to fill it out within 24 hours. Keep it super simple.

---

## The Form (Just 4 Questions)

### Question 1: Name
- **Type**: Short answer
- **Required**: Yes
- **Question**: "What's your name?"
- **Help text**: None (keep it clean)

### Question 2: Email
- **Type**: Short answer (with email validation)
- **Required**: Yes
- **Question**: "What's your email?"
- **Help text**: "We'll send your beta access link here"

### Question 3: Device Type
- **Type**: Multiple choice (single select)
- **Required**: Yes
- **Question**: "What device will you use?"
- **Options**:
  - iPhone/iPad (iOS)
  - Android phone/tablet
- **Note**: Keep it simple - just these two options

### Question 4: Agreement
- **Type**: Checkbox (single checkbox)
- **Required**: Yes
- **Question**: "Beta Agreement"
- **Checkbox text**: "I understand this is beta software and agree to provide feedback"
- **Note**: One simple checkbox, not multiple

---

## That's It! 

**Total time to fill**: ~1 minute  
**Questions**: 4 (all required)  
**Sections**: 1 (no sections needed, just questions in order)

---

## Form Settings

### General Settings
- **Collect email addresses**: Yes (automatic - Google Forms does this)
- **Limit to 1 response**: Yes (prevent duplicates)
- **Show progress bar**: Yes (helps show it's short!)
- **Shuffle question order**: No

### Presentation
- **Form title**: "FitAI Beta Signup"
- **Form description**: "Quick 1-minute form to get you set up! 🚀"
- **Confirmation message**: 
  ```
  Perfect! ✅ Got your info.
  
  As we ramp up towards launch, I'll be in touch soon with your beta access.
  
  Looking forward to sharing FitAI with you! 🚀
  ```

### Responses
- **Save responses to**: Google Sheets (automatic)
- **Get email notifications**: Yes (so you know immediately when someone submits)
- **Response destination**: Create a new spreadsheet called "FitAI Beta Testers"

---

## Quick Setup Steps (5 minutes)

1. **Go to Google Forms**: https://forms.google.com
2. **Create new form**: "FitAI Beta Signup"
3. **Add the 4 questions** in this exact order:
   - Name (short answer, required)
   - Email (short answer, required, with email validation)
   - Device Type (multiple choice: iOS or Android, required)
   - Agreement (checkbox, required)
4. **Set form settings** (see above)
5. **Test the form** yourself first (should take 30 seconds)
6. **Get shareable link**: Click "Send" → "Get link" → Copy link
7. **Add link to DM script** (replace `[YOUR_GOOGLE_FORM_LINK]`)

**That's it!** Super simple.

---

## Response Tracking

The Google Sheet will automatically have these columns:
- Timestamp
- Name
- Email
- Device Type
- Agreement (checkbox)

**Add these manual tracking columns to your spreadsheet:**
- Beta Link Sent (Yes/No)
- App Installed (Yes/No)
- Onboarding Complete (Yes/No)
- First Workout Logged (Yes/No)
- Status (Form Submitted/Beta Access Sent/Installed/Active/Stuck/Inactive)
- Notes

**That's it!** Simple tracking.

---

## Why This Works

✅ **4 questions only** - Takes 1 minute max  
✅ **All required** - No optional fields to skip  
✅ **No sections** - Just questions in order  
✅ **Clear purpose** - Name, email, device, agreement  
✅ **High completion rate** - Simple = more people finish  

**Everything else** (fitness goals, experience, etc.) can be collected during onboarding in the app itself. Don't make them do it twice!
