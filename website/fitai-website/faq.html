Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about fit.ai beta? We've got answers.

What is fit.ai?
fit.ai is an AI-powered fitness coaching app that helps you track workouts, get personalized insights, and chat with an AI coach that remembers your entire fitness journey.
How do I join the beta?
Fill out our waitlist form. We'll send your beta link via email. Access fit.ai via TestFlight (iOS) or direct download (Android).
What devices are supported?
Currently, fit.ai supports iOS (iPhone) and Android devices. Make sure you specify your device type when signing up for beta access.
Is the beta free?
Yes! The beta is completely free. As a founding member, you'll have early access to all features and help shape the future of fit.ai.
How do I report bugs?
Use the in-app bug reporting feature. You can also email us directly at emmanuelochiba7@gmail.com with detailed descriptions of any issues you encounter.
Will my data be reset?
During the beta period, we may need to reset data for testing purposes. We'll notify you in advance if this happens. Your feedback and workout history help us improve the app.
What features are available in beta?
Beta includes core features: workout logging, AI chat coaching, insights and analytics, calendar view, voice transcription, and personalized recommendations. Some features may be refined based on your feedback.
When is the public launch?
We're preparing for public launch soon. Beta testing will begin before the public launch, and waitlist members will get first access. We'll keep you updated via email as we get closer to launch.
Can I share beta access with friends?
Absolutely! Share your beta link with friends who are interested. The more people testing fit.ai, the better we can make it. We appreciate you spreading the word!
Is fit.ai a medical app?
No. fit.ai provides fitness information and coaching suggestions, but it is not medical advice. Always consult with a healthcare provider before starting any exercise program, especially if you have medical conditions.
When will I get beta access?
We'll send your beta link via email once you join the waitlist. You'll be among the first to access fit.ai. Questions? Contact us.
