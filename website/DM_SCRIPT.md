## Tracking Template

Create a spreadsheet with these columns:

| Name | Platform | Email | Form Submitted | Beta Link Sent | App Installed | Onboarding Complete | First Workout Logged | Status | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|----------------|----------------|---------------|---------------------|---------------------|--------|-------|
| John | iOS | john@... | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | Active | Loving it! |
| Jane | Android | jane@... | ✅ | ✅ | ⏳ | ⏳ | ⏳ | Pending | Waiting for install |

**Status Options:**
- `Pending` - Form not submitted
- `Form Submitted` - Form filled, waiting for beta access
- `Beta Access Sent` - Beta link sent, waiting for install
- `Installed` - App installed
- `Onboarding` - In onboarding
- `Active` - Using the app
- `Stuck` - Needs help
- `Inactive` - Not responding

## Tips for Outreach

1. **Keep it simple** - They already agreed, just need the form filled
2. **Build anticipation** - Use "as we ramp up" and "looking forward" language
3. **Create exclusivity** - Emphasize they're in the first group
4. **Be warm and friendly** - No need to sell, just ask nicely
5. **Send in batches** - Don't spam all 20 at once, do 5-7 per day
6. **Follow up gently** - If no response after 2 days, send a friendly reminder
7. **Be responsive** - Reply quickly to questions (builds trust)
8. **Track everything** - Use the spreadsheet to stay organized
9. **Celebrate wins** - When someone logs their first workout, celebrate it!
