## 📊 Logging Your First Workout

### How to Log a Workout:

1. **Tap "Log Workout"** (or use the menu)
2. **Add exercises** one by one:
   - Exercise name (e.g., "Bench Press")
   - Sets × Reps (e.g., "4 × 8")
   - Weight (e.g., "80 kg")
   - Optional: Rest time, notes
3. **Save** when done

### What Happens Next:

✅ **Instant Insights:**
- PR detection (personal records automatically flagged)
- Volume calculation (total weight lifted)
- Intensity level (light/medium/heavy/very heavy)
- Muscle groups worked

✅ **Calendar Update:**
- Your workout appears on your calendar
- PR badges show up automatically 🏆
- Color-coded by intensity

✅ **AI Learning:**
- FitAI remembers what you did
- Future recommendations get smarter
- Consistency tracking begins

## 📅 Calendar View

### Monthly Calendar
- **Green dot** = Completed workout
- **Yellow dot** = Light workout
- **Blue dot** = Planned workout
- **Grey** = Rest day / Missed

**Tap any day** to see:
- Workout details
- Exercises performed
- Volume lifted
- PRs achieved
- FitAI insights for that day

### Weekly Strip (Swipe to Navigate)

Above the calendar, you'll see a **horizontal scrolling strip** showing 7 days (Monday-Sunday):

```
Mon  Tue  Wed  Thu  Fri  Sat  Sun
🔥    🔥    -    🔥    -    🔥    -
Pull  Push  Rest  Legs  Rest  Arms  Rest
```

**Features:**
- **Swipe left/right** to see different weeks
- **Tap any day** to see workout details
- **PR badges** (🏆) on days with personal records
- **Intensity colors** (green = heavy, yellow = light)

**Pro Tip:** The strip always resets to the current week when you open the app, but you can scroll to see past or future weeks.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Best Experience

### 1. **Log Workouts Immediately**
- Log right after you finish (easier to remember)
- Don't wait until the end of the day
- More accurate = better insights

### 2. **Chat with FitAI Regularly**
- Ask questions, even small ones
- The more you chat, the better FitAI knows you
- Try: "What should I do today?" or "Am I overtraining?"

### 3. **Check Your Calendar Weekly**
- See your consistency at a glance
- Spot patterns (e.g., always skipping leg day)
- Celebrate streaks!

### 4. **Use Insights to Adjust**
- If FitAI says you're overtraining → take a rest day
- If it says you're imbalanced → add missing muscle groups
- If it detects a PR → celebrate! 🎉

### 5. **Be Honest**
- Log what you actually did, not what you planned
- FitAI learns from reality, not ideals
- Honest data = better coaching

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### **Q: Does FitAI work offline?**
A: Workout logging works offline (syncs when you're back online). AI chat requires internet connection.

### **Q: How does FitAI remember me?**
A: Deep Memory system stores your conversations, workouts, and preferences in a secure database. FitAI uses this to personalize every response.

### **Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Data export is coming soon! For now, all your data is stored securely and accessible through the app.

### **Q: What if I make a mistake logging a workout?**
A: You can edit workouts after logging! Tap any workout in your calendar or insights page, then tap the edit icon (pencil) in the top right. You can update exercise names, weights, reps, sets, notes, and more.

### **Q: How accurate is PR detection?**
A: PR detection compares your current workout to your historical data. It's automatic and gets smarter as you log more workouts.

### **Q: Can I use FitAI without a gym?**
A: Yes! Tell FitAI in onboarding or chat that you prefer bodyweight/home workouts. It will adjust recommendations accordingly.

### **Q: How often should I log workouts?**
A: Log every workout for best results. FitAI needs consistent data to provide accurate insights and track progress.

### **Q: What if FitAI gives advice I don't agree with?**
A: FitAI is a coach, not a dictator! You can always:
- Ask "Why?" to understand the reasoning
- Say "I prefer X instead"
- Override recommendations

FitAI learns from your preferences.
